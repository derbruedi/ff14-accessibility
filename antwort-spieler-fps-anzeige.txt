Anlass: "when I go to the settings for configure the fps limite, nvda say all the

Behoben in Version 5.88 (Release 2026-08-18).

=== ENGLISCH, zum Verschicken =========================================

Thanks for the report - I found it, and it is fixed in version 5.88.

What you ran into is the live frame rate counter that FFXIV draws inside the
System Configuration window itself. It sits in the corner of that window and
keeps counting on every one of the eight pages, which is why you heard it in all
settings and not only on the frame rate page. The mod announces changing texts in
that window, and because those announcements interrupt, the counter was cutting
off everything else NVDA was saying.

There was a filter meant to hide it, but it recognised the counter by the English
word "fps" and by whole numbers only. That depends on your client language and on
the exact format - a translated unit or a decimal value like "59.9" slips right
through it.

Version 5.88 replaces that with two rules that do not depend on any language:

- Any text in the configuration window that changes several times within a few
  seconds is treated as a live readout and is never spoken again.
- A changed text that starts with a digit is not announced either. You do not
  lose anything by this: the value of a control is still announced together with
  its name when you move onto it ("Strength, 100 %").

Update through the plugin installer or your Dalamud repository and it should be
quiet.

Two things would help me make sure it really was that counter and nothing else:

1. Which language does your game client run in?
2. If it still happens, please send me your log file. You find it at
   %APPDATA%\XIVLauncher\dalamud.log - just open the settings window, let the
   problem happen for a few seconds, close the game and send the file.

The same version also fixes something else in that window that you may notice:
the drop-down lists and sliders were being announced with the name of the
NEIGHBOURING setting, so you could change the wrong line without noticing. They
now read their real name.

=== DEUTSCH, nur zur Info fuer dich ===================================

Kurzfassung: Es ist die laufende Bildfrequenz-Anzeige IM Konfigurationsfenster
(im Dump Knoten id 4, Text "59 fps"), nicht eine bestimmte Seite. Sie laeuft auf
allen acht Reitern mit. Der alte Filter haing am Wort "fps" bzw. an einer reinen
Ganzzahl - beides sprach- und formatabhaengig. Neu: ein Text, der sich binnen
drei Sekunden dreimal aendert, gilt als laufende Anzeige und bleibt dauerhaft
stumm; zusaetzlich werden geaenderte Texte mit fuehrender Ziffer im
Aenderungs-Scanner nicht mehr angesagt. Welche der beiden Luecken bei ihm
zugeschlagen hat, wissen wir ohne sein Log nicht - deshalb die Rueckfrage nach
Sprache und Logdatei.
