Hello everyone,

I would like to introduce a project I have been working on: FF14
Accessibility, a plugin that makes FINAL FANTASY XIV playable with a
screen reader.

I am blind myself, and this plugin exists because I wanted to actually
play this game rather than read about it. It speaks through NVDA (via
Tolk) and also sends every announcement to a braille display. Its own
announcements are available in English and German.

Link:
https://github.com/derbruedi/ff14-accessibility


What it does

Menus and text:
- Title screen, character selection and the whole character creation,
  including the appearance step with its roughly twenty pickers. None of
  those pickers is text in the game, so a screen reader finds nothing
  there otherwise.
- Lists, dialogues, context menus and the journal are read row by row as
  focus moves.
- Settings windows with sliders, drop-down lists, checkboxes and tabs.
- The keybind window announces each command together with the key bound
  to it.
- NPC dialogue with the speaker's name first, plus the game's own error
  messages and toasts.

Chat:
- Incoming messages are spoken, and each channel can be silenced
  individually from the settings menu. A silenced channel merely stops
  being read aloud, it stays in the history for you to catch up on.
- While typing, the active channel is announced and every character you
  type is spoken, because the game's input field cannot be read.
- A history browser with eight separate categories and no message limit,
  so you can catch up at your own pace without disturbing the live chat.
- Enter in the history replies to the message you just heard, in the
  right channel. A tell is answered without typing the name.

Getting around:
- An object browser cycles through everything nearby with one key: NPCs,
  merchants, enemies, allies, other players, quest objectives, FATEs,
  levequests, gathering points, fishing spots, aetherytes, duty
  entrances and map waypoints. It announces name, kind, distance and
  direction, and targets the object at the same time.
- An audio beacon points at your target through stereo panning and
  pitch, with the volume following the distance.
- A walk guide talks you along the navigation mesh while you steer
  yourself, around obstacles, with waypoint tones and an arrival tone.
- Auto-walk goes to the target on its own. This needs the third party
  plugin vnavmesh, which the installer offers to fetch for you.
- Places the mesh does not know can be walked once by hand and recorded.
  If auto-walk gets stuck there later, it uses your own recorded trail
  instead of giving up.
- Your facing direction is announced as you turn, and you can copy your
  map coordinates to the clipboard or walk to coordinates somebody
  pasted in chat.

Combat:
- Your own HP as a number, target HP in steps, tones for HP and MP.
- Cycling through enemies announces their level and HP.
- A cast warning when an enemy casts at you, from any nearby enemy, not
  only your current target. Spells aimed at other players stay silent.
- An optional pulsing tone while you stand inside a telegraphed area
  attack, which stops the moment you step out.
- A tone and the name when an ability comes off cooldown.
- Experience gains and loot are announced, and loot rolls are read out
  with the item's stats so you can actually decide need or greed.

Items and hotbars:
- Bag, character window and armoury chest announce name, level and
  whether you can wear the piece, plus item level, defence and
  attributes. Jobs are named with your own classes instead of the game's
  list of abbreviations.
- If a piece belongs to a gear set, the announcement says so, because
  the game only paints that hint as a symbol onto the icon.
- An assignment menu places actions and items on any of the ten hotbars,
  entirely from the numpad.

Also covered: emotes, the hunting log, fishing, gathering, mounts, grand
company shops, exchange counters (item, price with its currency, and how
many you hold), the currency window (every row says which currency it is
instead of a bare number), the achievement window (your points and
certificates), character configuration, Triple Triad, and the deep
dungeons (Palace of the Dead and friends) with their rooms, treasure and
floor effects.


Installation

There is a graphical installer with a single button. It sets everything
up and keeps the plugin updated, so you never have to operate Dalamud's
own plugin window, which a screen reader cannot read.

1. Download FF14AccessibilityInstaller.exe from the latest release:
   https://github.com/derbruedi/ff14-accessibility/releases/latest
2. Run it. Windows SmartScreen may complain because the installer is not
   signed. Activate "More info", then "Run anyway".
3. Focus lands on the "Install or update" button. Press Enter and wait
   for the status messages.
4. Start XIVLauncher and log in. The plugin greets you with a spoken
   version announcement.

To update, run the same file again. The installer also updates itself
and asks before doing so.

You need Windows, FINAL FANTASY XIV and XIVLauncher with Dalamud, and
NVDA as your screen reader. The required Tolk DLLs ship with the plugin.


Honest notes

- This plugin runs on Dalamud/XIVLauncher, which is outside Square
  Enix's official terms of service. Use at your own risk.
- vnavmesh is an independent third party plugin by another author. It is
  only linked and downloaded, never redistributed here.
- Development and testing happen mostly on a German client, and I am one
  person playing one character. Some of the newer features have not been
  verified in game by anyone yet, and the release notes say which ones.
  If something announces nonsense, that is a bug and I want to hear
  about it.
- Six of the larger features were contributed by bladestorm360 on
  GitHub, and I am grateful for them.

Feedback, bug reports and feature requests are very welcome, either here
in this thread or as an issue on GitHub. If you try it and get stuck at
some point in the game, tell me where. That is usually how the next
feature gets built.

Thanks for reading.
